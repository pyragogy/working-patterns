# Roadmap

Working Patterns develops in evidence-first layers. Product complexity is earned by corpus maturity.

## Phase 0 — Method and seed · current

**Goal:** prove that the project can distinguish description, observation, support, counterevidence, and unknowns.

Delivered:

- Research Map 0.1 field reconnaissance;
- Research Map 0.2 deep evidence cycle;
- seven human organisational pattern families;
- claim-level evidence relations;
- case and source registries;
- initial licensing policy;
- zero-dependency integrity gate;
- Pyragogy AI pattern research agenda.

Exit criterion: the corpus survives revision without hiding previous interpretations.

## Phase 1 — Corpus hardening

Priorities:

- audit full texts behind the strongest C002 debrief claims;
- represent Study and Dataset as first-class records;
- add revision records for every material interpretation change;
- acquire failed, abandoned, and exit cases;
- measure implementation fidelity where cases permit it;
- extend licence verification;
- snapshot/version mutable web sources where legally and technically appropriate;
- add structured failure modes, boundary conditions, alternatives, outcomes, and costs.

Target is **not a pattern count**. Target is a corpus in which a reader can see why an assessment changed.

## Phase 2 — Broaden the human corpus

Candidate research areas:

- delegation with explicit domains;
- onboarding;
- organisational memory and decision logs;
- monitoring and graduated response;
- succession and founder exit;
- mutual-aid sustainability;
- commons governance;
- cooperative ownership/governance;
- open-source maintainer and contributor patterns;
- representative and deliberative processes;
- multilingual and low-resource groups.

The 200–400 candidate-pattern horizon remains a discovery pool, not a quota.

## Phase 3 — AI deep evidence cycle

Take the initial `WP-AI*` candidates through the same method as human patterns.

High-priority comparisons:

1. friction before AI delegation;
2. claim-level provenance and verification behaviour;
3. bounded agent mandates;
4. preservation of dissent in AI summaries;
5. multi-agent independence and false corroboration;
6. outcome-tracked AI advice;
7. human approval: meaningful control vs rubber-stamping.

The aim is to discover which “responsible AI” ideas survive operational testing.

## Phase 4 — Public knowledge interface

Only after the corpus is stable enough:

- browsable pattern pages;
- evidence and counterevidence views;
- genealogy graph;
- case explorer;
- machine-readable exports;
- stable release snapshots.

The site must not turn nuanced assessments into leaderboards or confidence scores.

## Phase 5 — Handbook

A Working Patterns handbook becomes an editorial distillation of the corpus, not the canonical source of truth.

Possible structure:

- Who decides?
- Who has power?
- Who knows?
- Who cares?
- Who belongs?
- Who can disagree?
- Who can change the rules?
- What can AI do?
- Who is accountable when AI acts?
- What happens when things go wrong?

The open corpus can remain inspectable while a paid handbook provides editorial value, synthesis, exercises, diagrams, and field use.

## Phase 6 — Evidence-aware MCP / advisor

The future service should answer situational questions, not return generic best practices.

It should:

1. identify the organisational problem;
2. ask for missing context;
3. retrieve candidate interventions;
4. expose evidence and counterevidence;
5. show boundary conditions and costs;
6. compare alternatives;
7. propose bounded local experiments;
8. record outcomes if the group chooses to contribute them.

A core prohibition:

> **The advisor must not causally recommend “the best pattern” when the corpus does not support that claim.**

## Commercial layer

Potential value layers can coexist:

```text
open research corpus
        ↓
editorial handbook / workbook / cards
        ↓
evidence-aware organisational advisor
        ↓
optional facilitation / research services
```

Commercialisation should fund curation and translation into usable tools, not close the evidence needed to inspect the recommendations.
