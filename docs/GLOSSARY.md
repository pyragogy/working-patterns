# Glossary

**Adoption** — A group declares or intends to use a practice.

**Implementation** — What the group actually does. Adoption and implementation may diverge.

**Implementation fidelity** — How closely observed practice matches the specified intervention.

**Pattern** — A recurring candidate response to an organisational problem, expressed precisely enough to investigate.

**Component** — A separable intervention inside a broader pattern family.

**Problem** — An organisational condition with observable consequences that an intervention attempts to change.

**Mechanism** — The proposed process connecting an intervention to an outcome. Mechanisms can be proposed, plausible, evidenced, or contested.

**Claim** — A bounded proposition that can be linked to evidence, counterevidence, and limitations.

**Evidence link** — A relation between a specific source/study and a specific claim.

**supports** — The source provides evidence in the direction of the claim within its stated scope.

**complicates** — The source narrows, qualifies, or reveals a trade-off without directly reversing the claim.

**contradicts** — The source provides evidence inconsistent with the specified claim or generalisation.

**context-only** — The source helps understand the problem, mechanism, or environment but does not directly test the claim.

**Case** — An identifiable empirical or reported organisational/research context used to understand implementation or outcomes.

**Boundary condition** — A condition that may change whether or how an intervention transfers or produces an outcome.

**Failure mode** — A describable way an intervention or its implementation can deteriorate. Failure modes must be labelled observed, reported, inferred, or hypothetical.

**Pattern maturity** — `candidate`, `documented`, `corroborated`, `contested`, `revised`, or `retired`. It is not a confidence score.

**Evidence scope** — Breadth of contexts directly supported by evidence: currently `narrow-context`, `multi-context`, or `cross-domain`.

**Unknown** — Not established by the current corpus. Unknown is not evidence of absence.

**Genealogy** — Historical/intellectual/organisational relationships between apparently similar patterns or evidence traditions.

**Independent confirmation** — Convergence that remains meaningfully independent across relevant authors, organisations, datasets, cases, and theoretical lineage.

**Pyragogy AI pattern** — A candidate socio-technical pattern governing how cognition, authority, evidence, memory, dissent, or action is distributed between people and AI systems. It is not merely a UI or software feature.
