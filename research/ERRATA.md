# Errata

Working Patterns preserves discrepancies in historical research outputs rather than silently rewriting them.

## ERR-001 — Research Map 0.2 claim count

**Detected:** 2026-09-14 during the repository integrity build.

### Historical statement

Research Map 0.2 states:

> “32 empirical/documentary claims and one methodological claim.”

That wording implies **33 total claims**.

### Enumerated registry

The Claim Registry displayed in the same report contains:

- C001: 4 rows
- C002: 7 rows
- C003: 4 rows
- C004: 4 rows
- C005: 5 rows
- C006: 4 rows
- C007: 4 rows

Total: **32 rows**.

`WP-C007-CL04` is the methodological/synthetic claim, so the enumerated table currently contains **31 source-linked empirical/documentary claims + 1 methodological claim = 32 total records**.

### Repository decision

The structured v0.1 corpus follows the **enumerated records**, not the prose total.

No 33rd claim is invented to satisfy the summary sentence.

The historical Research Map remains a snapshot; this erratum records the discrepancy separately.

### What could change this

If an omitted original claim is later recovered from the research process with a defensible proposition, provenance, and stable ID, it can be restored through an explicit revision rather than retroactively fabricated.

---

This erratum is a small example of the project's method: **when narrative and inspectable records disagree, preserve the disagreement and make the repair traceable.**
